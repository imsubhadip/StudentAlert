About the project

Project 18: Student Dropout Early Warning System

StudentAlert is a Django web application that helps an educational institution look at the students who may need extra help. A trained machine learning model predicts one of three outcomes for a student: Dropout, Enrolled or Graduate.

Teachers can add students, keep their details, run a prediction, and see the results on a dashboard.

The machine learning model
  • Algorithm: Random Forest classifier (scikit-learn), trained in Google Colab.
  • Dataset: UCI "Predict Students' Dropout and Academic Success" (4,424 student records).
  • Inputs: 30 features: admission details, personal and family details, fees, first-semester results and economic indicators.
  • Early warning: second-semester results were left out on purpose, so the model only uses information available after the first semester.
  • Data split: 80% of the records were used for training and 20% for testing.
  • Results: accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score are shown in the Colab notebook.

Dataset page: archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/697

Technologies used

Python, Django, SQLite, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap, Chart.js, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn and Joblib.

Please read: limitations
  • A prediction is an estimate, not a certain outcome.
  • The model sometimes misses students who really drop out, and sometimes gives false alarms.
  • "Enrolled" means the student had not finished the course at the end of its normal length.
  • The model was trained on data from one Portuguese institution. A college should check it with its own data before using it for real decisions.
  • Use the results only to offer extra help, never to punish a student.
  • Some inputs (such as gender, nationality and parents' details) are personal. Think carefully before relying on them.
